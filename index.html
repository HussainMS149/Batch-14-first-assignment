Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before joining us at DEVCONF 2026.

When and where is DEVCONF 2026?

DEVCONF 2026 runs March 12–14, 2026 at the Moscone Center in San Francisco, California. Doors open at 8:00 AM each day, with keynotes starting at 9:00 AM.

What's included with my ticket?

All tickets include access to keynotes, breakout sessions, expo hall, lunch, and networking events. PRO and TEAM tiers unlock workshops, VIP events, and extended session recordings. See the pricing section above for full details.

Can I get a refund if I can't attend?

Full refunds are available up to 30 days before the event. After that, you may transfer your ticket to a colleague at no charge or receive a 50% credit toward DEVCONF 2027.

Is there a virtual attendance option?

Yes. PRO ticket holders receive live-stream access to all main-stage sessions plus on-demand recordings. A standalone Virtual Pass ($199) is also available with 90-day recording access.

How do I apply to speak or sponsor?

Speaker CFP closes November 1, 2025 — submit at devconf.io/cfp. For sponsorship inquiries, email partners@devconf.io or visit our sponsorship page.

Are there student or diversity scholarships?

We offer 200 need-based scholarships covering full conference access. Applications open September 1, 2025 at devconf.io/scholarships. Recipients are notified by December 15, 2025.
